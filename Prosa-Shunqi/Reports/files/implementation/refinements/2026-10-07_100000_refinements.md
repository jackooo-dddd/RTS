## Translation

`Prosa/Implementation/Refinements/Refinements.lean`; it compiles with only the standard Lean axioms. 25 declarations
use no axiom, 11 use `propext`/`Quot.sound`, and the rest `propext`/`Classical.choice`/`Quot.sound`.

The CoqEAL and binary-number fragment has no Lean counterpart, so this file translates it first. Later refinements
files reuse it.
- **Rocq `positive`/`N`:**
  - Lean mirrors with the same constructors (`xI`/`xO`/`xH`, `N0`/`Npos`), and the same recursive algorithms as
    Corelib `BinNums.PosDef`/`NatDef` and Stdlib `BinNatDef`:
    - `Pos.succ`, the mutual `Pos.add`/`add_carry`, `pred_double`, `pred_N`;
    - the masks and the mutual `sub_mask`/`sub_mask_carry`;
    - `mul`, `compare_cont`, `compare`, `eqb`;
    - `N.sub`, `compare`, `leb`, `ltb`, `eqb`, `add`, `succ`, `pos_div_eucl`, `div_eucl`, `div`, `modulo`.
  - Their correctness against `Nat` is proved in Lean (e.g. `nat_of_bin_div_mod`, `mask_val_sub`).
- **MathComp's `nat_of_bin`/`bin_of_nat`:** MathComp's own definitions (`NatTrec.add`/`double`, `nat_of_pos`,
  `pos_of_nat`), with `bin_of_natK` proved.
- **CoqEAL:**
  - `Type`-valued relations stay `Type`-valued: `fun_hrel`, `hrespectful`, the class `refines`, and the parametricity
    inductives `bool_R`, `list_R`, `prod_R`;
  - `unify` is `Prop`-valued, as in CoqEAL;
  - the operation classes `zero_of` … `lt_of` are one-field Lean classes;
  - the `N` instances are those of `binnat`; `succN` and `Rnat` are translated as well.
- **Representation choices** (also in the spec's `lean_representation_note`):
  - Rocq's cumulativity `Prop ≤ Type` (an equation used as a relation) is Lean `PLift`;
  - the source's `Type`-valued `Global Instance`s and lemmas are Lean `def`s;
  - a Boolean `if` is Lean `if` on a `Bool`;
  - MathComp `last` is the helper `seq_last` (same recursion);
  - `iota` is `List.range'`, `size` is `List.length`, and `all p s` is `s.all p`;
  - `d %| m` is `decide (d ∣ m)`;
  - `\sum`/`\max` over sequences are the accepted `sumSeq`/`sumFiltered`/`maxFiltered`.
- **Binders** follow the elaborated source types. As in Rocq, each generic definition takes only the operation classes
  it uses: for example, `@predn_T : ∀ T, one_of T → sub_of T → T → T`.

- **Export:** 171,826 lines.
  - Root: the 16 definitions with their bodies and the 26 statements statement-only.
  - It also includes the accepted `div_ceil`, `shift_points_*` and `sumSeq`/`sumFiltered`/`maxFiltered`, and the
    accepted div/mod interface laws (with proofs).
- **Certificate:** one module, `RefinementsCorrespondence.v` (about 1,770 lines).
  - **Canonical maps.** The canonical maps from the Rocq values to the imported Lean mirrors have two-way roundtrips:
    unary numbers, `positive`, `N`, `mask`, `comparison`, Booleans, pairs and lists.
  - **Operations commute with those maps.** Each mirrored Stdlib/MathComp operation is proved to commute with the maps
    by structural induction. The imported recursions (`brecOn`) reduce by conversion, and the two recursions are the
    same.
    - Lean `Nat.div`/`mod`/`∣` use the accepted Euclidean argument through the exported interface laws.
    - `Nat.sub`, `pred`, `min`, `max`, `decide` and `List` `map`/`filter`/`zip`/`all`/`flatten`/`foldr`/`length`/
      `range'`, as well as `sumSeq`/`sumFiltered`/`maxFiltered`, are proved by induction or by a case analysis on the
      decision procedure.
  - **The 16 definitions** are related for related inputs:
    - generic element types are related by identity;
    - an operation-class instance is related to the single field of its Lean structure.
  - **The 24 `Type`-valued statements** are each related by a `TypeCorrespondence`: maps in both directions between
    the source statement and the target statement, taken with `type of`.
    - The maps are built from relation-level maps for `Rnat`, `bool_R`, `list_R`, `prod_R`, `refines` (through
      CoqEAL's `refinesE`/`refinesP`) and `unify`, plus the function correspondences above.
    - **Higher-order arguments** (`refine_foldr_lemma`) are related pointwise, never by function equality.
    - **Generic statements** (`refine_map`, `refine_all`, …, `refine_foldr_max`) share their element types and
      relations between both sides.
  - **The two `Prop` lemmas** are related by `PropSPropRel`.
- **No certificate uses its own source or target theorem.**
- **Audit:** 42 certificates. 33 are `CERTIFIED`, and 9 are `CERTIFIED_WITH_PROP_SPROP_FOUNDATION` (the
  `interpret_strict` foundation, used for divisibility, order and the two `Prop` lemmas).
  - `semantic_premises=[]`, `unexpected=[]` and no statement-only dependency;
  - the only other entries are the importer foundation (`propext`, `PrimInt63`) and definitional-UIP equalities;
  - `sorryAx` does not occur in the export.

## Translation

`Prosa/Analysis/Facts/Model/Overheads/PriorityBump.lean`. It proves three properties of priority bumps in an explicit-overhead uniprocessor schedule under basic readiness:
- **Preemption time:** a priority bump happens at a preemption time.
- **Arrival in the prefix:** inside a busy-interval prefix, a bump is caused by a job that arrived in the same prefix.
- **FIFO:** under a FIFO-like JLFP policy, no bump occurs in `(t1, t2]`.

The proofs follow the source:
- **First lemma:** case analysis on the jobs scheduled at `t - 1` and `t`. The job that starts at `t` gives a preemption time through the accepted `first_moment_is_pt`, with the overheads model a uniprocessor.
- **Second lemma:** the accepted `not_quiet_implies_exists_scheduled_hp_job_at_preemption_point`, then membership in `arrivals_between`.
- **Third lemma:**
  - an idle-to-busy bump contradicts the accepted `job_scheduled_in_busy_interval_prefix`;
  - a job-to-job bump contradicts `early_hep_job_is_scheduled`, since the earlier job is complete, via `completion_monotonic`, and so not pending.

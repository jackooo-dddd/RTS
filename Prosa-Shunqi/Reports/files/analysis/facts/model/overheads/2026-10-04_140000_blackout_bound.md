## Translation

`Prosa/Analysis/Facts/Model/Overheads/BlackoutBound.lean`. It bounds the blackout time (instants without supply) of an explicit-overhead schedule by the number of schedule changes:
- **Split:** blackout time splits into dispatch, context-switch and CRPD time.
- **One job:** without schedule changes, each total equals the time of one job (or idle) and is bounded by the respective overhead bound.
- **Counting:** a stretch without changes, or starting with its only change, has blackout at most `DB + CSB + CRPDB`; with `k` changes the bounds are `(DB + CSB + CRPDB) * k` (first change at `t1`) and `(DB + CSB + CRPDB) * (k + 1)` (changes counted from `t1 + 1`).

Binders follow the elaborated source types: every statement quantifies only over the schedule and, where used, the three overhead bounds. The source section's job-arrival, job-cost, policy and arrival-sequence hypotheses are not part of the elaborated statements.

The proofs follow the source in substance:
- **Split:** pointwise case analysis of the processor state under the interval sums.
- **One-job lemmas:** use the job scheduled at `t1` as the witness, with the accepted `no_changes_implies_same_scheduled_job`.
- **Counting lemmas:** use strong induction on the number of changes, with the accepted `first_schedule_change_exists`, `number_schedule_changes_cat` and `blackout_during_cat`. The first change after `t1` is split off (the source splits at the first change after `t1` by bounding a nested induction; the induction here is on the count directly).

Representation: a Boolean in `Prop` position is `= true`; `t1.+1` is `t1 + 1`. Lean axioms: only the standard ones.

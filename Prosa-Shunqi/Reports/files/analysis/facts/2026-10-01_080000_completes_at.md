## Translation

`Prosa/Analysis/Facts/CompletesAt.lean`. Binders follow the elaborated types.
- `\sum_(t1 <= t < t2) F t` is `∑ t ∈ Finset.Ico t1 t2, F t`.
- `\sum_(j <- s | P j) F j` is `sumFiltered s P F`, the accepted representation.
- Boolean summands are coerced by `Bool.toNat`.

Proofs follow the source:
- A job completing at `t > 0` gains service at `t - 1`, hence was scheduled then.
- A job completes at most once: induction on the interval end. A completion at `t2` means the job was not completed at `t2 - 1`, so, by completion monotonicity, no earlier instant was a completion.
- At most one job completes at a time: a filtered indicator sum over the duplicate-free arrivals is at most one, because two completing jobs were both scheduled at `t - 1` and the uniprocessor property makes them equal.
- Completion times are preemption times: time 0 via `zero_is_pt`; otherwise, a non-preemption time would keep the completed job scheduled (`neg_pt_scheduled_before`).
- No early higher-or-equal-priority job completes inside a busy prefix: the quiet time at the prefix start already completed it.

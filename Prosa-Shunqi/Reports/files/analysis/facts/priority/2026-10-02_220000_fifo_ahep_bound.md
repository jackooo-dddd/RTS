The lemma: under FIFO, within a busy interval `[t1, t2)` of a job `j` of `tsk`, the cumulative interference from other higher-or-equal-priority jobs in `[t1, t1 + Δ)` is bounded. The bound is the sum of the RBFs of the task set at `job_arrival j - t1 + 1`, minus `task_cost tsk`.

The proof follows the source:
1. The interference is the service of the other higher-or-equal-priority jobs.
2. That service is bounded by their workload.
3. The workload of the jobs arriving before `j`, other than `j` itself, is bounded by the task RBFs minus `j`'s own cost.

The last step uses the private helpers `job_cost_le_workload` and `sum_sub_le`.

The lemma: under JLFP scheduling on a restricted-supply uniprocessor, suppose `blocking_bound` bounds the service inversion and is maximal at `0`. Then `blocking_bound 0 + total_rbf L ≤ SBF L` bounds the busy intervals of `tsk` by `L`.

- The supply bound function is applied through its accepted class field.

The proof follows the source:
- **Workload bound:** the workload and interfering workload of a busy-interval prefix `[t1, t1 + L)` fit into `L`. The blackout is bounded through the busy SBF, and the service inversion through `blocking_bound`.
- **Case analysis:** either the prefix continues to `t1 + L`, or it terminates earlier. In both cases the accepted `busy_interval_is_bounded` / `terminating_busy_prefix_is_busy_interval` give the bound.

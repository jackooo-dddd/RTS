## Translation

`Prosa/Analysis/Abstract/RestrictedSupply/IwReadiness.lean`. All 18 statements are proved in Lean. The structure
follows the accepted RS `IwInstantiation.lean`.

**Interference split.** Four disjoint parts: blackout, another higher-or-equal-priority job, readiness-aware service
inversion, and supply without a ready higher-or-equal-priority job.
- The disjointness of another hep job and a service inversion is a local uniprocessor argument: two served jobs are
  equal.
- Without a ready hep job, the accepted readiness-interference facts apply.

**Hypotheses from schedule validity.** Arrival-before-execution and completed-jobs-do-not-execute are derived from
`valid_schedule` by the accepted facts.

**Abstract work conservation.** Uses the accepted `pending_hp_job_exists` and the classical work conservation.
- The ready hep job is either scheduled or backlogged.
- In the backlogged case, work conservation schedules some job.
- In both cases, the scheduled job must be `j`, since no other hep job is served.

**Representation.**
- The `readiness_interference_during` `Let` is the unfolded `Finset.Ico` sum of the counted predicate.
- The instances are named reducible definitions, passed explicitly.

Lean axioms: only the standard ones.

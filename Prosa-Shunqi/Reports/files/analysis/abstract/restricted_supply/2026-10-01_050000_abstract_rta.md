Proofs follow the source:
- **Blackouts:** a blackout inside the busy-interval prefix is interference. Otherwise work conservation gives service, and service implies supply.
- **Splitting interference:** interference splits pointwise, and hence cumulatively, into blackouts plus intra-supply interference. The blackouts are bounded by `Δ - SBF Δ` (`blackout_during_bound_SBF`).
- **After the threshold:** past the run-to-completion threshold the job is non-preemptive and so scheduled (`job_nonpreemptive_after_run_to_completion_threshold`). It then progresses whenever there is supply (`progress_inside_supplies`), so it incurs no intra-supply interference.
- **`IBF_P`:** the cumulative bound plus the intra-supply bound.
- **`IBF_NP`:** the source's three cases on `t1 + F` versus `t2` and `F` versus `Δ`. The middle case combines the fixpoint, the blackout bound, the split at `t1 + F`, the no-interference lemma and SBF-complement monotonicity.

### 2026-09-23 14:38 — direct computation-equation probe

I tested whether the validation-only `production_gcd_def` interface can avoid its imported `Nat.gcd_def` theorem dependency by replacing that proof with `rfl`. Lean 4.33.1 rejected the guard: `x.gcd y` is **not definitionally equal** to `if x = 0 then y else (y % x).gcd x`. The trial edit was reverted, and the production Lean definition was untouched. Thus this particular dependency cannot be removed by a bare kernel-reduction guard; it needs a separately justified arithmetic lemma or a different exact-artifact projection. The four theorem certificates remain conditional on the recorded statement-only arithmetic dependencies, and the whole file remains not accepted.

Splitting on `x` and trying `rfl` in each branch also failed; Lean's well-founded `Nat.gcd` remains nontrivial to reduce at this interface. Direct `simp [Nat.gcd]` hit a looping equation and maximum recursion depth, not a proof. These were validation-only probes and were reverted. The useful next route is a small explicit `Nat.gcd` equation proof from the imported well-founded definition/equations, with a fresh assumption audit; repeated reduction tactics are not a closure strategy.

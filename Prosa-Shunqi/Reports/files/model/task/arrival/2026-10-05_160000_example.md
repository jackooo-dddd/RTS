## Exact remaining blocker (checked 2026-09-30)

1. **The accepted closures contain no proved hint lemmas.** `periodic_as_sporadic.v` and `sporadic_as_curve.v`
   were accepted in *extract* mode (specs `model_task_arrival_periodic_as_sporadic`,
   `model_task_arrival_sporadic_as_curve`). The same holds for `curve_as_rbf.v` and `periodic.v`.
   - Their semantic sources contain `Definition statement_<lemma> : Prop := …`, not the lemmas. For example,
     `util/list.v` becomes the 179-line `ListSemanticSource`, versus 890 lines officially.
   - So no `basic_rt_facts` hint is ever registered.
   - The logical paths `prosa.model.task.arrival.periodic_as_sporadic` etc. do not exist in any accepted source
     closure.
   - A byte-identical compile of `example.v` against an accepted closure therefore fails at its first
     `Require Import`.
2. **The official proof closure does not compile in the pinned environment.**
   - `example.v` needs the full proof closure; `rocq dep` gives **55 files** (list and build summary archived in
     `Validation/logs/design_decisions_2026-09-30/example/`), including `util/all.v`, `analysis/facts/job_index.v` and
     `analysis/facts/sporadic/arrival_bound.v`.
   - Scratch build under `rocq93rc1` of a copy of the pinned tree:
     - all accepted source-compatibility patches that touch this closure were applied: `util-tactics`,
       `util-div-mod`, `util-seqset`, `analysis-schedule-prefix` and `analysis_facts_behavior_supply`;
     - it compiled `behavior/time.v`, `util/supremum.v` and `util/tactics.v`;
     - it then stopped at the **first proof-carrying file**:

     ```
     File "./util/list.v", line 23, characters 7-25:
     Error: Stack overflow.
     ```

     Line 23 is `Proof. by move=> x; elim. Qed.` (`last0_cons`). This is the known Rocq 9.3 `by`/`done` loop
     already recorded for other pinned proofs.
   - Getting the 55-file proof closure to compile would need proof-script compatibility patches across the official
     library: `util/*`, `behavior/*`, `analysis/facts/*` and the three conversion files. That is a
     source-compatibility programme, not a single-file fix.
   - The pinned environment is **not** to be changed (design decision 3), so a toolchain switch is not an option.
3. **Validator mode.** `Validation/scripts/validate_zero_declaration_aggregation.py` accepts a zero-declaration
   module only when its content is exclusively a `Require Export` list (its criterion 2).
   - `example.v` has `Require Import`s, a section and anonymous `Goal`s.
   - Even with a compiling closure, acceptance needs a new "Goal-only example" validator mode:
     - byte-identical source compiled on a verified proof closure;
     - Lean `example`s for the six goals, checked with the axiom audit;
     - nothing to certify, since there are no declarations.
   - No such mode exists, and none was added.

## What would unblock it

Both of the following are needed:
- (a) pinned proof closures for `periodic`, `periodic_as_sporadic`, `sporadic_as_curve` and `curve_as_rbf`, plus
  their transitive proof dependencies (starting with `util/list.v`), compiling under `rocq93rc1` via recorded
  proof-only patches;
- (b) a Goal-only validator mode.

Neither was started. No artifact was published, and no gate was changed.

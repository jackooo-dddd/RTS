## The Fin-sum decision (design decision 1 of 2026-09-30)

**Minimal reproduction.** Evidence is archived in `Validation/logs/design_decisions_2026-09-30/finsum/`.
- **Earlier diagnosis:** the attempt-1 export (311,459 lines) never finished importing (> 10 min at
  `Nat.Internal.Linear.ExprCnstr.denote_toNormPoly`). The diagnosis was that `∑ i : Fin n` / `Fin.fintype` pulls
  omega internals.
- **That diagnosis was wrong.** A declaration-level dependency graph of the export (`depgraph.py`, `paths2.py`) shows:
  - `Fin.fintype`, `multiproc_state` and the lemma type reach **no** `Nat.Internal.Linear` constant. They reach
    `Lean.Omega` constants, but so does every accepted Service export, and those import fine.
  - The only path into `Nat.Internal.Linear` was the proof of the validation-only equation `production_fin_sum`:
    `range_sum_foldr` → `Finset.sum_range_succ` → `Finset.notMem_range_self` → its `_proof_1_1` →
    `Nat.Internal.Linear.Expr`.
- **Two minimal exports, imported with the pipeline's `ulimit -s 65520`:**

| Export | Content | Rocq import |
|---|---|---|
| A | `def finSum n f := ∑ i : Fin n, f i` (with `Fin.fintype`) | **42 s** |
| B | only `Finset.sum_range_succ` (body theorem) | **did not finish**: killed after 1500 s, still at `Nat.Internal.Linear.ExprCnstr.denote_toNormPoly` |

**Existing mechanism, no exporter change.**
- `production_fin_sum` is already a checked propositional equation. It is exported with its full proof body and all
  proof dependencies (`body_theorems`), and certificates use it by transport, never as a definitional unfolding.
- Only its *proof* was replaced; its statement is identical:
  - `∑ i : Fin n, f i` is definitionally the list fold over `List.finRange n` (the carrier of `Fin.fintype`);
  - `map_finRange_eq_map_range'` then proves element-wise, by `List.ext_getElem`, that
    `(List.finRange n).map f = (List.range' 0 n).map (fun k => if h : k < n then f ⟨k, h⟩ else 0)`.
- The new closure has no `Nat.Internal.Linear` constant.
- No exporter code, normalization, `rfl` guard or projection was added or changed, and no proof field or
  statement-only assumption was introduced.
- The exporter binary is byte-identical to the one used by the accepted `Finset.Ico` interval-sum runs
  (`exporter_sha256` equal to the `analysis_facts_model_dynamic_suspension` run). So the interval-sum path is
  unchanged, and this certificate reuses it (`svc_interval_sum_related`).

**General `n`, including zero and one:**
- the certificate relation `mp_fin_sum_related` is proved for every related pair `nR ~ nL`;
- `mp_fin_sum_related_zero` and `mp_fin_sum_related_one` instantiate it at 0 and 1, and are audited;
- on the Lean side, `FinSumEdge.lean` checks the equation at `n = 0`, `1` and `3`.

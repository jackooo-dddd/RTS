\textbf{Lemma 2.} \ \textit{The workload bounds can be computed with}
\[
W_k^{\mathrm{NC}}(\tau_i,x)
=
\left\lfloor \frac{x}{T_i} \right\rfloor \cdot C_i
+
[x \bmod T_i]^{C_i}
\tag{5}
\]
\[
W_k^{\mathrm{CI}}(\tau_i,x)
=
\left\lfloor \frac{[x-C_i]_0}{T_i} \right\rfloor \cdot C_i
+
C_i + \alpha
\tag{6}
\]
\textit{where}
\[
\alpha = \bigl[[x-C_i]_0 \bmod T_i - (T_i - R_i)\bigr]_0^{C_i-1}.
\]

\textit{Proof:} Similar to reasoning in [6], [14], see Figure 3.

\textbf{Lemma 4.} \ \textit{Given a $\varphi \geq 0$, let $\chi$ be the minimal solution of the recursive equation}
\[
x=\left\lceil \frac{\Omega_k(x)}{M} \right\rceil + C_k.
\tag{11}
\]

\textit{Then $\chi-\varphi$ is an upper bound of $\tau_k$'s response time with this particular $t_0=r_k-\varphi$.}

\bigskip

\noindent
\textit{Proof:} Suppose the real worst-case response time of $\tau_k$ with $t_0=r_k-\varphi$ is $R$, and assume $\chi-\varphi<R$ for the sake of contradiction. Since $R$ is the real WCRT, there is a job sequence in which a job $J_k$ exhibits this response time, i.e.,
\[
f_k-r_k=R.
\]
It follows:
\[
\chi<R+\varphi=f_k-r_k+\varphi=f_k-t_0
\]

Thus, Lemma 3 applies, and therefore (10) holds with $x=\chi$. This contradicts the assumption of $\chi$ being a solution of (11). \hfill $\blacksquare$

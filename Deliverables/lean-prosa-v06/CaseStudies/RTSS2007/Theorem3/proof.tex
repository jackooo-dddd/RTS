\textbf{Theorem 3} \ \textit{A task $\tau_k$ has a response time upper bounded by $R_k^{ub}$ if}
\[
\sum_{i \neq k} \min \left( I_k^i(r_k^*, r_k^* + R_k^{ub}), R_k^{ub} - C_k + 1 \right)
< m(R_k^{ub} - C_k + 1)
\]

\bigskip

\noindent
\textbf{Proof:} If the inequality holds for $\tau_k$, from Lemma 1 we have
\[
I_k(r_k^*, r_k^* + R_k^{ub}) < (R_k^{ub} - C_k + 1)
\]
therefore $J_k^*$ will be interfered for at most $R_k^{ub} - C_k$ time units. From the definition of interference, it follows that $J_k^*$ (and therefore every other job of $\tau_k$) will complete at most at time $R_k^{ub}$. \hfill $\blacksquare$

\textbf{Theorem 3. (RTA-CE)} \ \textit{The upper bound of WCRT of task $\tau_k$ is}
\[
R_k=\max_{T^{CI}\in\mathcal{Z}}\left(R_k^{T^{CI}}\right)
\tag{18}
\]

\textit{Proof:} As long as $\tau_k$ is released at one of its critical instants, its response time is then upper bounded by $R_k$, which, according to Theorem 1, is the upper bound of $\tau_k$'s worst-case response time. \hfill $\blacksquare$

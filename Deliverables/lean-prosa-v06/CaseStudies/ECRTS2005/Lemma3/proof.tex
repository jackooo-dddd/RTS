\paragraph{Lemma 3.}
\textit{The interference that a task $\tau_k$ can suffer in interval $[a,b]$
is the sum of the interferences of all other tasks (in the same interval)
divided by the number of processors:}
\[
	I_k(a,b) = \frac{\sum I_{i,k}(a,b)}{m}.
\]

This descends from the observation that EDF is work-conserving, thus in the
time instants in which a job is ready but not executing, each processor must be
occupied by a job of another task.

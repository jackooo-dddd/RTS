\noindent
\textbf{Theorem 18.6}\quad \textit{Theorem 18.4 and 18.5 are equivalent when $n \leq 2m$.}

\medskip

\noindent
\textit{Proof}\quad From Lemma 18.1, we know that both theorems are equivalent when $n \leq m$.
Now, consider the tasks with priorities from $m+1$ to $2m$. The interference from the $m$
highest priority tasks $\tau_i$ can be computed considering that $\forall\, 1 \leq i \leq m:\ R_i^{\mathrm{ub}} = C_i$. In
these conditions, the term $\widehat{W}_i^{CI}(L)$ given by Eq.~(17.7) is equal to the corresponding
term $\widehat{W}_i^{NC}(L)$ given by Eqs.~(17.8), for all $1 \leq i \leq m$, so that $\widehat{W}_i^e(L) = 0$ for all
$1 \leq i \leq m$. Out of the first $2m-1$ tasks, at most $2m-1-m=m-1$ of them
can have $\widehat{W}_i^e(L) > 0$. Limiting the sum of the $\widehat{W}_i^e(L)$ terms in Eq.~18.5 to the largest
$m-1$ contributions has therefore no effect for each of the $2m$ higher-priority tasks.
Hence, Eq.~18.5 reduces to Equation~(18.4), proving the theorem. \hfill $\square$
